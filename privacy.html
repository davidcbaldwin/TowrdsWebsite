Privacy

Last updated October 2026

What this site collects

This site does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers, and it does not collect your personal information directly.

Services this page connects to

To work, this page loads content from a few outside services. When it does, those services may receive standard technical details such as your IP address and browser type:

  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces.
  • Zoom hosts the video, the Towrds 10 application, and the meeting scheduler. Anything you submit in the application or scheduler goes to Zoom and to Towrds, and is covered by Zoom's privacy policy.
  • LinkedIn hosts the Towrds newsletter and David's profile.

How Towrds uses what you send us

Information you share in the application or when booking a call is used only to evaluate fit and respond to you. Towrds does not sell or share it.
