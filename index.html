The questions owners don't always ask.

Straight answers before you apply.

Will you work with my competitors?

It's possible. GovCon is a small world, and with ten clients, some may compete in the same markets. Nothing crosses between clients: no information, no insights, no strategy. That's in writing in the NDA and Consulting Services Agreement.

Who sees my numbers and my plans?

You, your leadership team, and me. That's it. Everything is covered by a Consulting Services Agreement and an NDA, both signed before we start.

How long is the commitment, and how is it billed?

$10K a month, on a 12-month base year, then option years. Each renews automatically unless either of us gives written notice at least 90 days before the period ends, which gives us time to prep your next Annual Reset. The first payment is due at signing. After that, it's a monthly ACH pre-payment due on the 1st, and we send a formal invoice five business days before each month ends. The first 30 days are covered by our money-back guarantee.

How much of my time does this take?

About 5 hours a month in the regular rhythm: a 90-minute Bi-Weekly Tactical and a 1-hour advisory session every two weeks. On top of that, one day each quarter for the Quarterly Shift, a 2-day Annual Reset once a year, and 60-minute performance reviews for your team twice a year. Averaged out, roughly 2 hours a week.

How is this different from an EOS implementer or a BD consultant?

An implementer teaches you a non-GovCon business system and leaves your team to run it. A BD consultant works one slice of the business. Towrds puts a Chief Strategy Officer in the seat, running a GovCon-built system with your team every quarter across leadership, execution, and growth.

What if it isn't working?

If either of us isn't feeling it at any point in the first 30 days, we just say so. You get your money back and we walk away friends.
